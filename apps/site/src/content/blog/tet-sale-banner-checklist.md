Tết is the busiest selling season of the year and the one with the least slack. Artwork needs to be ready before the shopping window opens, not during it, because everyone's designer is booked and every hour spent fixing banners is an hour not spent on stock and shipping. Here is the checklist we give sellers who use Oia for seasonal campaigns.

## 1. Fix the date, then work backwards

Tết falls in late January or early February, and the exact date moves every year: check the lunar calendar for the year you are planning, then decide when the campaign starts. Marketplace traffic ramps up one to two weeks before, and the last delivery-before-Tết promise is usually your strongest hook. Write the dates down before designing anything; a beautiful banner with a wrong date is a reprint.

## 2. Start from the seasonal pack, not a blank page

Oia bundles a [Tết sale pack](../../templates/packs/tet-sale/) alongside 34 other templates. Red silk, gift boxes, festive reds, with the copy as editable text layers. The point is not that the template is sacred; it is that repricing stays free. Seasonal campaigns live on repricing: early-bird price, final-week price, last-day price. If those numbers are text layers, each change costs nothing. If they are painted into a generated image, every change is a repaint.

## 3. If you generate, write Vietnamese in the prompt and proofread twice

The generation loop reads Vietnamese end to end: describe the offer in Vietnamese, state the exact price and wording you want rendered, and the banner copy comes back with full diacritics. Two honest warnings. First, always proofread the rendered copy, accents included; it is your banner, the model does not sign it. Second, when you supply a real product photo as a reference, check the result against the photo. Repaints can shift packaging details, and on a gift-box campaign that means the box, the ribbon, the logo.

## 4. One design, many sizes

The same campaign usually needs a shop avatar, a feed banner and a livestream cover. Build the artwork once and export at each marketplace size rather than redesigning per slot; sizes for Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop are built in. The [prompt library](../../prompts/) keeps tested prompts per size if you are generating fresh artwork.

## 5. Dry-run the repost

Before the campaign goes live, open every export at full size once. Check the date, the price, the diacritics, the product, the safe zones if it is an overlay. Thirty seconds of looking beats a reprint after the post is scheduled.

That is the whole checklist: dates early, templates over blank pages, Vietnamese proofread twice, one design across sizes, and one last look before posting. Chúc bạn selling season thật thuận lợi.
