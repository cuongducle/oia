The overlay loop

01 — PICK

Overlay preset or template

Livestream overlay 1080×1920 transparent, or start from Flash sale price / Freeship badge / Countdown frame.

02 — GUIDE

Safe zones on

TikTok and Shopee Live guides mark where the platform's buttons and comments land — keep your card out of the hatched bands.

03 — REPRICE

One prompt, same style

Price drop mid-stream? Ask for the new number — the repaint keeps the glossy card treatment, previewed before it replaces the layer.

04 — EXPORT

Transparent PNG

Guides never reach the exported bytes — a clean alpha PNG for OBS or your streaming tool.

Why it works

Community-measured zones

TikTok & Shopee Live layouts, honest label — not an official spec, measured where the UI actually lands.

Text that stays editable

Template prices are native text layers — swap $8.90 → $5.90 for free, no regeneration.

3:4 refused, honestly

Ask for a wrong-ratio overlay and Oia tells you instead of silently shipping a stretched card.

Live covers too

Shopee live cover 500×500 and TikTok cover 1080×1920 presets ship alongside the overlay.

What size is a TikTok livestream overlay?

1080×1920 (9:16). Oia ships the preset with a transparent background and community-measured safe-zone guides so your card never hides behind TikTok's buttons and comments.

Do the overlays export with transparency?

Yes — PNG with alpha at 1080×1920, straight into OBS. The guides stay in the editor and never reach the exported bytes.
