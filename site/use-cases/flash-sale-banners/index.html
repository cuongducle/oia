Use case — e-commerce

Flash-sale banners that look designed — and change in one prompt.

Describe the sale, pick the marketplace size, and the copy is rendered into the artwork with glossy 3D typography. When the price moves, you don't rebuild anything — ask for the repaint and the style stays.

From idea to live banner

01 — DESCRIBE

One sentence, right size

"Milk-tea flash-sale banner, 20% off today" + the Shopee 1:1 chip. The planner writes the copy into the image prompt — English or Vietnamese, your call.

02 — GENERATE

Typography baked in

≈ 1 image on your own provider quota. Headline, discount, urgency line — rendered as part of the artwork, not flat overlay text.

03 — REPRICE

@ the layer, ask for the repaint

Select the banner, type the new number. Oia sends exactly that image, locks the dimensions, previews the result — nothing touches your page until you apply.

04 — EXPORT

Marketplace-ready

PNG or JPG at the preset size, PDF and PPTX for the whole campaign. Undo keeps the previous version one step away.

How do I change the price on an AI banner?

Select the banner layer and ask: change '20% OFF TODAY' to '30% OFF TODAY' — keep everything else the same. Oia regenerates with the new number in the same style, previews it, and replaces the layer only when you apply.

What sizes are supported?

Shopee 1:1 (1024), Lazada 1:1 (800), TikTok Shop 1:1 (1080), plus posters, 9:16 story sizes, presentations and A4.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free and open source (MIT). Generations use your own provider — a ChatGPT plan via the bundled Codex CLI, or your OpenAI/fal API key. Every action shows its cost before you run it.
