Flash sale: questions

How do I change the discount on a flash sale template?

Click the price layer, type the new number. The typography treatment stays exactly as designed, that is the difference between an editable layer and baked pixels.

Can I turn a flash sale template into a countdown?

Add a date or timer text layer in the editor, or generate a fresh countdown frame with the livestream overlay presets.
