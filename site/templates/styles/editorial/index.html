Editorial templates: questions

What makes a template “editorial”?

Three laws the design system enforces: a serif display headline with a quiet italic subhead, a warm paper background with real texture (never flat), and restrained color. A critic inside the app scores generated images against these laws.

Can the editorial look carry Vietnamese text?

Yes, the app ships Be Vietnam Pro with full diacritics, and several templates already carry Vietnamese sample copy (phở poster, café menu).
