Menu — questions

Can I add more items to the menu?

Yes — duplicate the item list layer or add a new text box; the scene is a real canvas, not a flat image.

Do menu templates print well?

Export at PNG/PDF in the app at full resolution; the artwork ships at 1024×1536 and scales cleanly to boards.
