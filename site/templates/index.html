Template FAQ

Are the templates really free?

Yes — Oia is MIT-licensed open source and every bundled template ships inside the app. No account, no credits.

Can I edit the text in a template?

That's the point — headlines in the studio templates are native text layers, not baked pixels. Change the wording, font, size and color for free, no regeneration.

Can I save my own designs as templates?

Yes — Share ▸ Publish as template puts the current scene in your library, ready to reuse on the next campaign.
