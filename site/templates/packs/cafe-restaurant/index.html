Café & restaurant pack: questions

What is a pack, exactly?

A curated bundle: templates that share a niche, plus the provenance, the real generation prompt behind every AI plate, so you can rerun the look on your own dishes.

Can I regenerate a plate with MY food?

Yes, copy the provenance prompt into the composer, describe your dish where the prompt names the plate's subject, and pick the editorial style preset.
