Product launch pack: questions

Do the packs cost anything?

No, the app and every template are free and open source. The pack pages exist so launch-week has one link with everything in order.

How do I reuse a plate's look for my product?

Each AI plate lists its provenance prompt. Paste it into the composer, swap the subject (sneaker → your product), keep the composition clauses, and pick the same style preset.
