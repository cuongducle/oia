Layers, not magic

The image and the design are different things.

Every Oia document is built from objects (text, images, shapes, your own uploads) on separate layers. Move the headline without touching the photo. Fix a price without paying for a new image.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

Is Oia really free?

Yes, MIT-licensed open source. No account, no credits, no subscription. Generations run on your own provider: sign in with ChatGPT through the bundled Codex CLI, or plug in your OpenAI/fal API key. Your provider's quota and pricing still apply.

Can I edit the text in a generated image?

By default the copy is rendered into the artwork for the best-looking typography. Change it by asking, “make it 30% off today”, and the repaint keeps the style. Need movable letters instead? Flip Editable text right away before generating, or run Make text editable on any design.

Do I have to start from a blank canvas?

No, a studio library ships inside the app: flash sales, menu boards, product drops, posters, social posts, livestream overlays and more. Every template is a real, editable scene, and starting from one costs zero images.

What do Research and Auto refine do?

Before designing, the planner can research current facts (dates, prices, trends) and shows every finding with its source. After painting, auto refine checks each image against your brief, spelling and language of the rendered text first, and re-generates when it finds a fixable defect. Both are bounded and priced before you run.

Mac or Windows?

Both. macOS builds are Apple Silicon (M1 and newer), Windows builds are 64-bit (Windows 10/11). Your projects live on your machine.

Do I need an API key?

No. The recommended path is zero-setup: open Runtime in the app, sign in with ChatGPT, and generate against your plan quota. Keys are optional (OpenAI, fal).

What sizes does it support?

Shopee / Lazada / TikTok Shop squares, TikTok 9:16, story and post ratios, livestream overlays (1080×1920 transparent), posters, 16:9 presentations, YouTube thumbnails and A4.

Where do my designs live?

On your machine, projects, assets and conversations are stored locally. Nothing is uploaded to us (there is no “us” server). Prompts and images still go to the provider you configure.
