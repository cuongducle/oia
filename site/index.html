FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

Is Oia really free?

Yes — MIT-licensed open source. No account, no credits, no subscription. Generations run on your own provider: sign in with ChatGPT through the bundled Codex CLI, or plug in your OpenAI/fal API key.

Can I edit the text in a generated image?

By default the copy is rendered into the artwork for the best-looking typography. Change it by asking — “make it 30% off today” — and the repaint keeps the style. Need movable letters instead? Flip Editable text right away before generating, or run Make text editable on any design.

What do Research and Auto refine do?

Two agent loops that run over your provider. Research the web (on by default where supported) lets the planner look up current facts — dates, prices, trends — before designing, and shows every finding with its source. Auto refine checks each generated image against your brief — spelling and language of the rendered text first — and re-generates when the critic finds a fixable defect. Bounded, and the full price is shown before you run (e.g. up to 2 images + 2 reads). Both switches remember your choice.

Mac or Windows?

Both — native builds for macOS (Apple Silicon) and Windows. Your projects live on your machine.

Do I need an API key?

No. The recommended path is zero-setup: open Runtime in the app, sign in with ChatGPT, and generate against your plan quota. Keys are optional (OpenAI, fal).

What sizes does it support?

Shopee / Lazada / TikTok Shop squares, TikTok 9:16, story and post ratios, livestream overlays (1080×1920 transparent), posters, 16:9 presentations, YouTube thumbnails and A4.

Where do my designs live?

On your machine — projects, assets and conversations are stored locally. Nothing is uploaded to us (there is no “us” server).
